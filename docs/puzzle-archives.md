# Historical puzzle archives

Browse `/games/archives`, linked from the Connections library. Connections opens in the existing player without Supabase. Mini supports typing into the grid, selecting clues, keyboard navigation, checking answers, revealing and resetting. Download links expose each year's normalized games and the distinct answer/word lists.

## Daily play and history

The Daily shelf links to Wordle, Mini, Connections, Spelling Bee, Krillion and Anthropeum. Wordle lives at `/games/wordle`; old Fivefold URLs redirect with their selected date intact. `/games/archives` covers all four NYT games, with date navigation and downloadable year-by-year puzzles and answer lists. Wordle validates guesses against the 14,855 accepted words in the current published NYT client; `scripts/sync-wordle-words.py` refreshes the list and records its source URL and hash. No NYT login is needed for the ordinary public dated endpoints checked on 2026-11-19.

Published NYT puzzles load by exact publication date from the normal NYT JSON endpoints, with validated dates, server-side durable snapshots and exact-date bundled fallbacks. There is no rotating answer list, different-day fallback, or future NYT preview. A daily cron captures the four current puzzles; requests also fetch new dates on demand. A source failure without an exact snapshot produces an unavailable message.

As of 2026-11-19: Wordle has 1,980 puzzles from 2021-06-19; Bee has 3,119 from 2018-05-06; Mini has 4,474 from 2014-08-21; Connections has 1,257 from 2023-06-12. All six picture-based Connections dates retain the original picture tiles, accessible labels, difficulty order and tile positions. Bee 2018-06-30 is unavailable because its published answer list conflicts with its published center letter; it is not silently repaired. The archive manifest records source URLs, date coverage, unavailable records and normalized hashes.

## Game mechanics and scores

Every daily resets at midnight Pacific time and plays the original's puzzle for that same date, so games whose originals reset on Eastern time lag them by a few hours. Each game keeps your first finished result, on this device and on the server; Spelling Bee is the exception, since its score grows as you find words. Logged times round to the nearest second. Results save before syncing and retry on reconnection. Old local-scoring rows are preserved as a separate scoring version and can be inspected on the leaderboard; they do not compete with original-rule results. Date navigation and historical practice remain supported. Future previews stay unranked.

- Mini: active solve time, manual pause and automatic pause when leaving; resume behind a hidden-grid overlay. The timer starts with Start, stops on completion, and resets when the grid is cleared. The first unassisted completed time, rounded to the nearest second, is the ranked result; checks, reveals and autocheck mark assistance. Tab/Shift+Tab, direction arrows, Space, Backspace, pencil mode and scoped square/word/puzzle help are supported. Lower time wins.
- Connections: four groups, four mistakes, one-away feedback, repeat-guess protection, four-column layout, and retained selection after a wrong guess. Solved puzzles rank by time, fastest first; unsolved ones rank below them by groups found and never win the day. Mistakes do not break ties.
- Wordle: the original's 6 tries, word list, marks and hard mode (on only before the first guess, can be turned off mid-game). No clock. Ranked by guess count; a loss counts as 7 guesses in averages. Hard-mode results show `*`.
- Spelling Bee: exact source answer lists, center-letter validation, four-letter minimum, original word/pangram points and rank thresholds. Form controls no longer receive game keyboard shortcuts. Revealing a past/practice hive persists and ends word entry.
- Krillion: same-date original prompts and a 25-second clock per prompt with no preview (the original adds 3 seconds first), drawn like the original's sonar ring and draining bar, pink for the last 3 seconds. Invalid guesses can be retried while the clock runs; at expiry the current draft is judged once. The clock runs on wall time, so reloading never grants a new timer. Answers are judged on the device from each prompt's answer key, fetched 1 prompt ahead, with the server as fallback. Matching follows probes of the original (57 of 59 agree): accents, case, punctuation and a leading "the" are ignored, spaces count; an exact answer or alias scores at once; from 5 letters, the closest answer or alias within min(3, 0.28 × its length) edits, or the same text without spaces, comes back as `“typed” → “answer”` and scores when sent again or when time runs out. Ties go to a canonical answer, then list order. The original's accepted answers, aliases and six rarity scores (10/15/30/60/85/100) are used; the maximum is 700. The final screen shows the original's player curve (live while the original's date is open, then the last copy saved, including a capture at 04:55 UTC just before it closes), dive log, score bands, rounds, logbook and next-dive countdown. No AI judging is used.
- Anthropeum: original public artifacts and historical polygons, 250-year guess intervals snapped to the original's 125-year steps (3000 BCE to 1750 CE), nearest polygon-edge distances (0 points with no region, as in the original), and original exponential scoring (up to 5,000 location plus 5,000 time points per artifact, 100,000 overall). The map supports pan, zoom, pinch and keyboard placement. The last round's feedback is shown before final results.

Krillion's original November 17 review was observed after completing one normal run; the local scorer reproduces that run's 90-point result. The current-day public review can also be captured without submitting additional games. The bundled compressed key contains 110,153 accepted answers across seven prompts. The cron captures the public current-day prompts and review at 05:10 UTC into server-only storage. It never uses the paid archive or silently substitutes another date. Earlier days without a captured original key are unavailable rather than approximated with a made-up answer bank.

## Refresh and verification

`python3 scripts/sync-nyt-archives.py --through YYYY-MM-DD` resumes the direct NYT archive download without requesting future days. `--verify-existing` rechecks saved dates. The older mirror import scripts remain available for source recovery. Add `krillion` to save a current original answer key locally. `npx tsx scripts/capture-krillion-review.ts` captures the current review into the configured server database. Neither submits game results to Krillion.

`tests/original-mechanics.test.ts` covers timers, original-key scoring/aliases, interval and boundary math, ranking direction and Bee rules. `e2e/daily-games.spec.ts` exercises full play, invalid-answer retries, timeout submission, pause/reload, scoped assistance, post-game reviews and score delivery. `scripts/verify-daily-results.ts` verifies database versions, immutable first Mini times, Connections tiebreaks and group separation using an isolated group removed afterwards.

Mechanics references: [NYT Mini help](https://thenewyorktimeshelpcenter.helpjuice.com/360025912452-The-Mini-Crossword), [Connections help](https://thenewyorktimeshelpcenter.helpjuice.com/360011158491-New-York-Times-Games/28525912587924-Connections), [Spelling Bee help](https://thenewyorktimeshelpcenter.helpjuice.com/360011158491-New-York-Times-Games/23675493312404-Spelling-Bee), original Krillion gameplay/review and Anthropeum's published client scoring.

## Remembered player and game surfaces

Games share the existing device ID with Daily Log and Notes. A name entered through the pencil control is stored locally and on the server, and restored with the long-lived device cookie if local storage is cleared. Future results carry the device ID; future note/log device stamps and reader comments reuse the name. Editing the name does not rewrite earlier results. The leaderboard retains optional group selection. Existing saved daily-game names migrate automatically.

Daily game headers put Home, the title, leaderboard and help on the same row. Krillion uses a pixel ocean surface and VT323 typography; Anthropeum uses its parchment/burgundy palette, serif typography, map, artifact photograph viewer and timeline within sfjc.dev. NYT game surfaces use white backgrounds, sans-serif controls and the games’ colors. These are local playable implementations, not embedded upstream pages. The November 17 Anthropeum collection is bundled for durable replay.

## SET

`/games/set` provides a deterministic daily board of twelve classic cards containing exactly six sets. Cards use red, green and purple, the oval/diamond/squiggle shapes, and solid/striped/open shading. These are generated sfjc.dev puzzles, not fetched original daily boards; the original puzzle endpoint was unavailable when this was implemented. Dates, active-time pause/resume, saved found sets, duplicate detection and assisted practice are supported. Unassisted completions join the common leaderboard; equal set counts compare completion time, with the first completed time retained.

`/games/triples` provides the full 81-card solo game and PIN rooms. Room mutations use optimistic version checks so simultaneous claims cannot both score the same cards. Late polling responses cannot replace a newer board. Room membership survives reload; leaving transfers host control and removes an empty room. The existing cleanup cron also removes stale rooms.

The remaining name-recovery fixes separate untouched legacy lobby fallbacks from user-edited drafts, so a cookie-restored name can replace a stale lobby name. Existing saved game names also replace old Notes device labels. Tests cover both migrations, all six daily leaderboard flows, the SET rules/deck, daily generation, full solo completion and two-player synchronization. Classic card features follow the [publisher’s SET rules](https://www.playmonster.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/SETRules.pdf).

Room actions also require a private per-seat credential returned only to the joining player; public roster IDs do not authorize claims or host actions. Temporary storage failures return 503 and retain the local seat, while only a genuine 404 clears it. An explicit invite to a different room opens that lobby without discarding the previous saved membership. Server and browser regressions cover these cases.

Successful room entry normalizes the invite URL, including when creating a different room from an invite. The credential protocol is versioned; uncredentialed pre-release rooms are explicitly retired rather than granting ownership from public IDs. There were no existing rooms at the rollout check, and no room data was deleted for this transition.

## Ranking and presentation

The leaderboard separates Daily results, Standings and Your stats, sharing one game selector and date picker. Calendar and trend details appear in Your stats. Daily winners use completion time for Connections and Mini, guess count for Wordle, total word points for Bee, and total score for Krillion and Anthropeum. Equal results share the rank. Incomplete Connections runs never win. Changed Bee/Wordle puzzle assignments use a new scoring version, and source-date mismatches are kept out of current standings. Earlier results remain available separately.

Anthropeum uses the published 250-year interval and exponential distance/time formulas, requires explicit location and period selections, and shares three no-cost time/place hints across all ten rounds. The time hint is the original millennium containing the artifact's midpoint year. Photographs support thumbnails, enlargement and zoom. Krillion retains its seven rounds, 25-second clock, accepted-answer sets and rarity tiers, with the original ocean/HUD/prompt/answer-bar layout cues. Neither game submits results to its upstream service.
